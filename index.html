About

Background

I started in film and game narrative, shipped a commercial mobile RPG, then spent my MFA building physical-digital experiences. Product design is where those threads meet.

Design the loop, not just the screen. I map what brings someone back tomorrow before I draw a single flow.

Test with real people early. Beta cohorts, live observation, and short surveys shape my priorities more than my own taste.

Prototype in the real medium. I build working prototypes with AI-assisted code, so feedback comes from something people can actually use.
